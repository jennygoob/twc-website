The Three Appointments

Every Step. No Surprises.

Each appointment has a specific purpose. Nothing is rushed, nothing is skipped, and you always know what is coming next.

1
Appointment One
Meet. Draw. Begin.

The Consultation + Blood Draw

This is a conversation, not a sales pitch. Your provider wants to understand what you are experiencing, what you have tried before, and what your goals are. No pricing is discussed. No pressure applied. After the conversation, we draw blood for 42 biomarkers across 10 body systems. Same day, same visit.

What Happens

Symptom review, health history, goals discussion, followed by a full 42-biomarker blood draw.

Duration

Approximately 45 minutes for the consultation and blood draw combined.

What to Bring

Come fasting if possible. Bring a list of current medications and supplements.

What Happens Next

Blood sent to the lab. Results typically take 5 to 7 business days. We schedule Appointment 2.

2
Appointment Two
The Moment Everything Clicks

Your Diagnostic Report

This is where most patients say they finally understand what is going on. Your provider walks you through every finding in your CLARITY Diagnostic Report. Patterns your previous provider never connected. Hormones that explain the symptoms you have been living with. A clear recommendation for which tier fits where you are right now.

The Report

Health signals, detected patterns, biological age analysis, and system-by-system scoring across all 10 body systems.

Who Presents

Joe or Janya walks you through every page. Dawn reviews all labs behind the scenes before this appointment.

Tier Recommendation

Based on your findings, your provider recommends the CLARITY tier that matches your optimization needs.

Decision Point

You decide which path forward makes sense. No rush, no expiration. Your report is yours regardless.

3
Appointment Three
Built for You. Adapts with You.

The Deep Dive

This is where Dawn, our lead provider, meets you in depth for the first time. She has already reviewed your labs and read your Diagnostic Report. Now she builds the rest of your protocol: treatment plan, nutrition protocol, and medication impact analysis. This is the session where your living protocol is born.

Treatment Plan

A complete, personalized treatment protocol built from your biomarkers, symptoms, and health goals.

Nutrition Protocol

Targeted nutrition guidance built around your specific deficiencies and metabolic patterns.

Medication Impact

Analysis of how your current medications interact with your biomarkers and new protocol.

Living Protocol Begins

From this point forward, your protocol adapts continuously based on how your body responds.

Appointment 3 is exclusive to Elite and Transformation members. Core members can book provider visits a la carte.

Behind the Scenes

What Happens Between Appointments

The time between your blood draw and your Diagnostic Report is not idle. This is where the analysis happens.

01

Lab Processing

Your blood work is processed by a certified laboratory. 42 biomarkers across 10 body systems are analyzed. Results are typically ready within 5 to 7 business days.

02

Provider Review

Dawn personally reviews every marker. She identifies patterns, connections between systems, and optimization opportunities that a standard reference-range check would miss entirely.

03

Report Generation

Your CLARITY Diagnostic Report is generated: health signals, detected patterns, biological age analysis, system scoring, and a personalized tier recommendation.
