Common questions about CLARITY

What biomarkers does CLARITY test?

We analyze 40+ biomarkers across 10 body systems: hormone signaling, metabolic health, thyroid function, liver function, lipid health, inflammation, nutrient levels, blood health, kidney function, and immune markers. This goes far beyond the 10-marker panel your PCP typically orders.

Do I need a referral?

No. CLARITY is a direct-access program. Book a free consultation and we'll handle everything — no referral, no prior authorization needed.

How quickly will I see results?

Most members notice changes in energy, sleep, and mood within 4-8 weeks. Biomarker improvements typically show on your second lab panel. Every protocol is built for sustainable, long-term optimization — not quick fixes.

What if I'm already on hormone therapy?

Many CLARITY members come to us already on HRT from another provider. We'll review your current protocol, run a full panel, and optimize from there. Bringing existing prescriptions is common.

Is this covered by insurance?

CLARITY is a membership-based program and is not billed through insurance. Many members use HSA/FSA funds.

What's the difference between tiers?

All tiers include the full 40+ biomarker panel and Diagnostic Report. Core is prescription management. Elite adds quarterly visits with Dawn, 24hr concierge messaging, a static protocol dashboard, and general lifestyle recommendations. Transformation is concierge-level with monthly visits, weekly adaptive protocol updates, personalized meal and workout plans, wearable and CGM integration, real-time feedback, and peptide credits.
