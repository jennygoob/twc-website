After Your Visit

Your first visit is the beginning, not the decision point. Here is what the process looks like once you leave the clinic.

Before You Call

The questions we hear most from people who are thinking about reaching out.

Is the consultation actually free?

Yes. The initial phone call and in-person consultation are completely free with no obligation. If you decide to move forward with the blood draw at Appointment #1, the lab fee is $300. That fee is credited toward Elite or Transformation enrollment.

How long does the first visit take?

Plan for about 30 to 45 minutes. That includes meeting the team, walking through your health history, and the blood draw if you choose to do it the same day. No rush.

Do I need to prepare anything?

If you plan to do the blood draw same-day, fasting for 10 to 12 hours gives us the most accurate panel. Bring a list of current medications and supplements if you have one. Otherwise, just show up.

What if I am already on hormone therapy?

Many CLARITY members come to us already on HRT from another provider. We will review your current protocol, run a full panel, and optimize from there. Bringing existing prescriptions is common and expected.

Do I have to choose a tier at the consultation?

No. Tier selection happens at Appointment #2 after you have seen your Diagnostic Report and understand what your biomarkers reveal. The consultation is purely informational. No pressure, no enrollment forms.
