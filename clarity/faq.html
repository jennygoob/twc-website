Labs & Testing

What We Test. Why It Matters.

The specifics of what gets measured, how often, and why it goes beyond a standard panel.

42
Biomarkers
42 biomarkers across 10 body systems: hormone signaling (testosterone, estradiol, progesterone, DHEA-S, SHBG), thyroid cascade (TSH, free T3, free T4, antibodies), metabolic health (glucose, insulin, HbA1c), lipid panel, inflammation markers (hs-CRP, homocysteine), nutrient levels (vitamin D, B12, folate, magnesium, iron studies), liver function, kidney function, blood health (CBC), and immune markers.
Because isolated markers miss the story. Your thyroid function affects your hormones. Your cortisol affects your thyroid. Your inflammation affects your cortisol. Testing 10 markers is like reading one chapter of a book. Testing 42 across 10 systems lets us see the connections and patterns that drive how you actually feel.
Lab frequency depends on your tier. Core members get periodic panels as needed. Elite members receive 2 comprehensive panels per year. Transformation members receive 4 panels per year. Follow-up labs are how we measure response and adjust your protocol.
Optimal ranges. Standard reference ranges are built from population averages that include unhealthy and elderly individuals. A testosterone level of 350 might be in range, but it is not optimal for a 42-year-old. We optimize to functional ranges based on your age, sex, and health goals.

Standard care checks 10 markers. CLARITY checks 42. That is 4x the data, across 10 interconnected body systems, analyzed by a provider who has been doing this for 24 years.

Treatment

BHRT, Peptides, Protocols, Side Effects

What treatment looks like inside CLARITY, how protocols are built, and what to expect.

Bioidentical hormones are structurally identical to the hormones your body naturally produces. Unlike synthetic hormones, they bind to receptors the same way your endogenous hormones do. At CLARITY, every prescription is guided by lab data and monitored through regular panels. Dosages are adjusted based on your response, not a generic formula.
Peptides are short chains of amino acids that signal specific biological functions: recovery, fat metabolism, immune regulation, tissue repair. They are available as add-ons across all tiers. Transformation members receive a monthly peptide credit as part of their membership. Peptides are sourced from licensed compounding pharmacies.
Most members notice improvements in energy, sleep quality, and mood within 4 to 8 weeks. Full optimization, including body composition, cognitive clarity, and libido, typically takes 3 to 6 months of protocol adjustments guided by follow-up labs. This is not a quick fix. It is a recalibration.
When properly monitored, bioidentical hormone therapy has a strong safety profile. Potential side effects vary by hormone and dosage, which is why we run regular labs and adjust your protocol based on your response. Common early adjustments include energy fluctuations and sleep pattern changes as your body recalibrates. Your provider monitors everything.
Many CLARITY members come to us already on HRT. We will review your current protocol, run a full panel to see where you actually stand, and optimize from there. Bringing existing prescriptions is common and expected. We do not start from scratch if there is a foundation to build on.
