The story behind the practice

In 2002, Dawn Philp opened her first practice — not because the system was working, but because it wasn't.

After years in conventional medicine, she watched patients follow every protocol… and still get worse. And over time, she began to recognize a pattern she couldn't ignore:

One prescription leading to the next — each one masking a symptom while quietly creating another. A domino effect where nothing was truly resolved. And before long, it became a cycle patients were told they'd be on for the rest of their lives.

Something was broken.

Then it became personal.

Her daughter, Jennifer, was living proof.

From a young age, she dealt with persistent issues — weight struggles, gut imbalances, histamine reactions, even shingles at 20. Not life-threatening, but persistent. Unresolved. And dismissed by every provider as “normal.”

Instead of accepting each symptom at face value, Dawn did what most providers won't — she stepped outside the system and rebuilt her entire approach from the ground up.

Why could one person handle stress, food, or environmental triggers with no issue… while another couldn't?

Why do some bodies adapt… while others break down?

What was missing at the foundation?

What imbalance or deficiency was being overlooked?

That wasn't a question the conventional system was built to answer.

That wasn't good enough.

So Dawn did what most providers won't — she stepped outside the system and began rebuilding her entire approach from the ground up. Root cause. Not symptom management. Real answers, not temporary relief.

Jennifer grew up inside that process — watching every protocol challenged, every assumption questioned, every breakthrough earned.

And over time, she didn't just get better — she understood why.

Together, they built THE WELLNESS CO. — a practice grounded in one belief:

You don't stop at symptoms.

And you don't stop until you find the answer.
