Treatment Zones

Every Zone Has Its Own Language

Each area of the face has its own anatomy, its own aging pattern, and its own filler requirements. Your provider selects the product and technique specific to each zone.

  • Cheeks

    Restores midface volume that diminishes with age, lifting the entire lower face. Creates structural support that reduces nasolabial folds and under-eye hollows without treating those areas directly.

    Structural Foundation
  • Lips

    Enhances volume, defines the vermillion border, and corrects asymmetry. Our approach prioritizes proportion over size — lips that complement your facial structure rather than dominating it.

    Precision Placement
  • Jawline

    Creates definition along the mandibular border for a sharper, more angular profile. Addresses jowling, weak chin projection, and the loss of definition that blurs the jaw-to-neck transition.

    Structural Contouring
  • Under-Eyes

    Corrects tear troughs and dark hollows beneath the eyes. This is one of the most technique-sensitive filler areas, requiring precise product selection and placement depth to avoid puffiness.

    Advanced Technique
  • Nasolabial Folds

    Softens the deep lines from nose to mouth corners. Often best addressed with cheek augmentation first, allowing the fold to lift naturally before direct treatment.

    Fold Reduction
  • Chin

    Projects the chin forward for improved facial balance and profile harmony. A recessed chin can make the nose appear larger and the jawline less defined — filler corrects this without surgery.

    Profile Balancing
  • Temples

    Fills the temporal hollowing that creates a gaunt, aged appearance. One of the earliest signs of facial volume loss and one of the most overlooked. Restoration creates a fuller, more youthful frame around the eyes.

    Facial Frame

Dermal Fillers FAQ

How long do dermal fillers last?

Most hyaluronic acid fillers last between 6 and 18 months depending on the product, treatment area, and your metabolism. Areas with more movement (like lips) metabolize filler faster than structural areas (like cheeks). Your provider recommends the right product longevity for your goals.

Are fillers painful?

Most patients describe mild discomfort rather than pain. Modern fillers contain lidocaine, which numbs the area as the product is injected. Topical numbing cream is applied beforehand for sensitive areas like the lips. Most treatments take 15 to 30 minutes.

What filler brands do you carry?

We use the Juvederm and Restylane families of hyaluronic acid fillers. Each includes multiple products for different areas and depths — from lightweight Volbella for perioral lines to robust Voluma for deep cheek augmentation. Your provider selects based on your anatomy, treatment zone, and goals.

Can fillers be reversed?

Yes. Hyaluronic acid fillers can be dissolved with an enzyme called hyaluronidase. This is one of the key safety advantages of HA fillers over other types. If you are unhappy with a result or experience a complication, the product can be partially or fully reversed — usually within hours.

Who is a good candidate?

Most adults experiencing volume loss, structural changes, or facial asymmetry are good candidates. Your provider evaluates your anatomy and medical history during consultation. Fillers are not recommended for those who are pregnant, breastfeeding, or have active skin infections in the treatment area.
