What is NAD+?

NAD+ (Nicotinamide Adenine Dinucleotide) is a coenzyme found in every living cell. It is essential for mitochondrial energy production, DNA repair, gene expression, and hundreds of enzymatic reactions that keep your cells functioning. NAD+ levels decline significantly with age — by age 50, most people have roughly half the NAD+ they had at 20. This decline is linked to fatigue, cognitive decline, accelerated aging, and reduced cellular repair capacity.

Energy

Mitochondrial Fuel

NAD+ is required for the electron transport chain — the primary mechanism your mitochondria use to produce ATP (cellular energy). More NAD+ means more efficient energy production at the cellular level.

Repair

DNA Protection

NAD+ activates sirtuins and PARP enzymes — the cellular repair machinery that fixes DNA damage, regulates inflammation, and protects against age-related degeneration.

Cognition

Brain Function

The brain consumes disproportionate amounts of NAD+ for neurotransmitter production and neural signaling. Restoring NAD+ levels supports cognitive clarity, focus, and memory.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is NAD+?

NAD+ (Nicotinamide Adenine Dinucleotide) is a coenzyme found in every cell of your body. It is essential for cellular energy production, DNA repair, and hundreds of metabolic processes. NAD+ levels decline significantly with age — by age 50, most people have roughly half the NAD+ they had at age 20.

What is the difference between NAD+ IV and injection?

NAD+ IV delivers a higher therapeutic dose directly into the bloodstream over 60-90 minutes, providing maximum cellular saturation. NAD+ injections are quick intramuscular shots that deliver a maintenance dose in under five minutes. IVs are recommended for initial loading and deep treatment protocols; injections are ideal for weekly maintenance between IV sessions.

How often should I get NAD+ therapy?

For initial loading, many patients start with a series of IV sessions over 1-2 weeks. Maintenance typically involves monthly IV sessions, weekly injections, or a hybrid of both. Your provider will recommend a schedule based on your goals, age, and individual response.

What does NAD+ therapy feel like?

During an NAD+ IV, some patients experience mild flushing, warmth, or brief chest tightness that resolves quickly by adjusting the drip rate. These sensations are normal and temporary. Most patients report feeling mentally clearer and more energized within hours of their first session. Injections are quick, well-tolerated, and produce no notable side effects.
