Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a vitamin injection take?

Most vitamin injections take under 5 minutes. They are intramuscular shots, not IV infusions, so there is no extended sit time. You can walk in, get your injection, and continue your day immediately.

What is the difference between injections and IV therapy?

Vitamin injections are quick intramuscular shots that deliver a single nutrient or small combination in under five minutes. IV therapy delivers larger volumes of multiple nutrients directly into the bloodstream over 30-60 minutes. Injections are ideal for targeted, quick-hit nutrient delivery. IVs are better for comprehensive, multi-nutrient protocols.

How often should I get injections?

Frequency depends on the injection type and your individual needs. B12 and amino blends can be done weekly. NAD+ and glutathione are commonly scheduled weekly or biweekly. Your provider can recommend a cadence based on your lab results and health goals.

Do I need to be a CLARITY member?

No. Vitamin injections are available to all patients as a standalone service. However, CLARITY members receive 10-20% off all injections and get recommendations informed by their actual lab results — so your injection protocol targets the nutrients your body actually needs.
