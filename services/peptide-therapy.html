Peptide Therapy FAQ

Are peptides safe?

Yes. Peptides are naturally occurring amino acid chains that your body already produces. Our protocols use pharmaceutical-grade peptides from licensed compounding pharmacies, administered under direct NP supervision with ongoing monitoring. Side effects are generally minimal and temporary.

How are peptides administered?

Most peptides are administered via subcutaneous injection using a small insulin-type needle. Some are available as oral capsules or nasal sprays. Your provider will determine the best delivery method, and we provide complete training on self-administration.

How long until I see results?

Timeline varies by peptide. Healing peptides like BPC-157 can show improvement within the first week. Cognitive and metabolic peptides typically show measurable results within 4-8 weeks. Full protocol cycles run 8-12 weeks.

What's the difference between peptides and steroids?

Peptides are signaling molecules that work with your body's natural processes — telling cells to repair, grow, or release hormones. Steroids directly alter hormone levels by overriding them. Peptides result in fewer side effects and a more sustainable long-term approach.

Do I need lab work first?

Not always required for standalone therapy, but we strongly recommend it. Baseline labs let your provider select the most effective peptides for your specific biology. CLARITY members receive recommendations informed by their full 42-biomarker panel.
