Frequently Asked Questions

How many sessions do I need?

Most conditions respond well to 6-12 sessions, typically scheduled 1-2 times per week. Your provider will recommend a treatment plan based on your specific condition and severity. Some acute injuries resolve faster, while chronic or degenerative conditions may benefit from extended protocols.

Does SoftWave hurt?

Most patients describe a mild tingling or pulsing sensation during treatment. The intensity can be adjusted based on your comfort level. No anesthesia is required, and the treatment is well-tolerated across all age groups.

How soon will I see results?

Some patients notice improvement in pain and mobility after the first session. However, SoftWave works by triggering a biological healing cascade — stem cell migration, blood vessel formation, and tissue remodeling — that develops over 4-12 weeks. Results continue to improve between sessions and after completing your treatment series.

Is SoftWave covered by insurance?

SoftWave is typically not covered by insurance. We offer package pricing for multi-session treatment plans. CLARITY members receive tiered discounts of 10-20% on all sessions.

How is SoftWave different from other devices?

SoftWave uses patented unfocused (parallel) acoustic wave technology, which treats a larger tissue volume per pulse than focused or radial devices. This broader coverage activates more stem cell migration and blood flow with fewer sessions. It is also the only acoustic wave device with FDA 510(k) clearance for the specific claims of connective tissue activation and improved blood supply.
