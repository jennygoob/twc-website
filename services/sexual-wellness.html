The Hormonal Connection

Many Intimate Concerns Are Hormonal

Low testosterone in men and women, estrogen decline during perimenopause and menopause, and hormonal imbalances at any age are among the most common — and most overlooked — causes of decreased libido, arousal difficulties, vaginal dryness, and intimate discomfort.

Procedural treatments like Forma V and PRP address the physical tissue directly. But for many patients, lasting improvement requires addressing the hormonal factors as well. That is where our CLARITY program provides a foundation that procedural treatments alone cannot.

The combination of hormonal optimization and targeted procedural treatment produces the most complete, sustained results.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Forma V?

Forma V is a non-invasive radiofrequency treatment for vaginal rejuvenation. It delivers controlled thermal energy to internal and external tissue to improve tightness, moisture, comfort, and overall intimate wellness. There is no surgery, no downtime, and the treatment is performed in a private clinical setting by our NP team.

What is PRP enhancement?

PRP (Platelet-Rich Plasma) enhancement uses your own blood-derived growth factors to stimulate tissue regeneration, increased sensitivity, and improved function. We draw a small amount of blood, process it to concentrate the platelets and growth factors, and inject the PRP into the treatment area. We offer PRP enhancement for both men and women.

Are these treatments private and confidential?

Absolutely. All sexual wellness consultations and treatments are conducted in private clinical rooms by our NP-led team. We approach intimate health with the same professionalism and discretion as any other medical service. Your information is protected under standard medical privacy practices.

Can hormones affect sexual wellness?

Yes. Low testosterone (in both men and women), estrogen decline, and broader hormonal imbalances are among the most common causes of decreased libido, arousal difficulties, and intimate discomfort. Our CLARITY program identifies and treats these hormonal root causes, which can be combined with procedural treatments for comprehensive results.
