Frequently Asked Questions

How long does an IV session take?

Most infusions take 30-60 minutes. NAD+ infusions may take longer depending on the dose — typically 60-90 minutes for a full session. You can relax comfortably in a private setting during the infusion.

Is IV therapy safe?

Yes. All infusions are supervised by our NP-led clinical team using medical-grade compounds sourced from licensed compounding pharmacies. Every patient is assessed before their first infusion to ensure safety and appropriate dosing.

How often should I get an IV?

It depends on your goals. Some patients benefit from weekly sessions during acute phases (illness recovery, athletic training), while others maintain optimal levels with monthly infusions. CLARITY members follow a schedule calibrated to their actual lab results.

Do I need to be a CLARITY member?

No. The Drip Lab is open to everyone. However, CLARITY members receive priority scheduling, tiered discounts of 10-20%, and biomarker-informed protocols — meaning your formulation is tailored to your actual lab results rather than general symptoms.
