The Technology

HIFEM Energy

Muscle Construction

High-Intensity Focused Electromagnetic energy induces supramaximal contractions — the kind your nervous system physically cannot produce on its own. These contractions force muscle fibers to adapt, remodel, and grow. The equivalent of 20,000 crunches or squats, concentrated into 30 minutes of progressive overload your body has never experienced.

Fat Disruption

The same contractions that build muscle also trigger fat cell apoptosis in the treatment area — programmed cell death of adipocytes. Your body metabolizes and eliminates these disrupted fat cells naturally over the following weeks. The result: more muscle mass and less subcutaneous fat from a single treatment modality.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does Emsculpt work?

Emsculpt uses HIFEM (High-Intensity Focused Electromagnetic) technology to trigger supramaximal muscle contractions — contractions far more intense than what voluntary exercise can achieve. A single 30-minute session induces 20,000 contractions, building and strengthening muscle fiber while triggering fat cell apoptosis in the treatment area.

How many sessions do I need?

Most patients see optimal results with 4-6 sessions, typically scheduled 2-3 days apart. Results build progressively as muscle fibers repair and strengthen. Maintenance sessions monthly or quarterly sustain and build upon gains depending on your goals.

Does Emsculpt hurt?

Most patients describe the sensation as an intense muscle contraction — similar to a very strong workout. The intensity is adjustable and gradually increased during the session. There is no pain during or after treatment, though mild muscle soreness similar to post-exercise soreness is common for a day or two.

What areas can Emsculpt treat?

Emsculpt treats the abdomen, glutes, arms (biceps and triceps), thighs, and calves. Each area receives a targeted protocol optimized for the muscle group being treated. Your provider will recommend the best approach for your body composition goals.

Is this a weight loss treatment?

No. Emsculpt is a body composition treatment. It builds muscle and reduces targeted fat, but it is not designed as a standalone weight loss solution. For patients with significant weight to lose, we recommend starting with medical weight management and introducing Emsculpt once the fat layer has been reduced — so you can sculpt and define the muscle underneath.

Can I combine Emsculpt with other treatments?

Absolutely. Emsculpt pairs well with medical weight management (GLP-1 medications for fat loss first), peptide therapy (MOTS-C and AOD-9604 for metabolic enhancement), SoftWave for tissue recovery, and CLARITY hormone optimization for maximized muscle synthesis and fat reduction. Stacking accelerates results.
