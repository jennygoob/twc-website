What is Thermography?

Thermography is a non-invasive imaging technique that uses a high-resolution infrared camera to detect and map heat patterns on the surface of your body. These thermal patterns correspond to blood flow, metabolic activity, and inflammation — giving your provider a physiological snapshot that reveals changes often invisible to other imaging methods. It is completely passive: the camera reads heat your body naturally emits.

Safe

No Radiation

Thermography uses passive infrared detection. There is no radiation exposure, no injections, no dyes, and no compression. It is safe for all patients, including pregnant women.

Early

Early Detection

Thermal imaging can detect physiological changes — inflammation, blood flow shifts, metabolic irregularities — before they develop into structural problems that show on traditional imaging.

Complete

Full-Body Insight

Thermography can scan any region of the body — breast health, musculoskeletal systems, thyroid, digestive, vascular, and neurological patterns are all visible in thermal data.

Applications

What Thermography Reveals

  • Inflammation patterns — Joint, soft tissue, and organ-level inflammation
  • Circulatory changes — Vascular irregularities and blood flow asymmetry
  • Musculoskeletal issues — Nerve compression, muscle strain, referred pain patterns
  • Breast health screening — Adjunct tool for monitoring thermal changes over time
  • Thyroid activity — Thermal asymmetry indicating thyroid dysfunction
  • Treatment tracking — Objective visual evidence of treatment progress over time

Thermography is a complementary screening tool. It does not replace mammography or other diagnostic imaging.

The Process

How a Thermography Scan Works

No radiation. No contact. No discomfort.

01
Prepare

Acclimation

You will spend a few minutes in a temperature-controlled room to allow your body to reach a stable thermal baseline. Avoid sun exposure, exercise, caffeine, and hot showers before your appointment.

02
Scan

Thermal Imaging

Our infrared camera captures high-resolution thermal images of the targeted body regions. The camera does not touch your body — it passively reads the heat you emit. The scan itself takes approximately 15-30 minutes.

03
Review

Interpretation

Your thermal images are analyzed by a certified thermography interpreter who identifies patterns, asymmetries, and areas of concern. Results are reviewed with you and integrated into your clinical care plan.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does thermography detect?

Thermography detects thermal patterns associated with inflammation, circulatory changes, musculoskeletal issues, nerve dysfunction, and metabolic irregularities. It is also used as an adjunct screening tool for breast health. It shows physiological changes rather than structural ones, making it useful for early detection before symptoms develop.

Is thermography safe?

Yes. Thermography uses passive infrared detection — it reads heat your body naturally emits. There is no radiation, no contact, no compression, and no injections. It is completely non-invasive and safe for all patients, including pregnant women.

Does thermography replace mammography?

No. Thermography is a complementary screening tool, not a replacement for mammography. It detects physiological changes (blood flow, inflammation) rather than structural changes (masses, calcifications). Many patients use both as part of a comprehensive approach to breast health. Always follow your provider's recommendations for mammography screening.

How should I prepare for my appointment?

Avoid sun exposure, hot showers, exercise, caffeine, and topical products (lotions, oils, deodorant) on the day of your scan. These can alter your thermal patterns and affect accuracy. Wear loose-fitting clothing to your appointment. We will provide specific preparation instructions when you schedule.
