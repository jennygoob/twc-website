Our Story

The system wasn't working.
So she rebuilt it.

In 2002, Dawn Philp opened her first practice — not because the system was working, but because it wasn't. After years watching patients follow every protocol and still get worse, she recognized a pattern she couldn't ignore: one prescription leading to the next, each one masking a symptom while quietly creating another.

Then it became personal. Her daughter Jennifer was living with the proof — weight struggles, gut imbalances, histamine reactions, even shingles at 20. Not life-threatening, but persistent. Unresolved. And dismissed by every provider as "normal."

Together, they built THE WELLNESS CO. — a practice grounded in one belief: you don't stop at symptoms, and you don't stop until you find the answer.
