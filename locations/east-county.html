Your Neighborhood Clinic

Established in East County Since 2002

This clinic was not planted here by a corporate office. It grew here. Dawn Philp opened the doors on Mission Gorge Road over twenty years ago with a single treatment room and a conviction that wellness should be personal, thorough, and led by the provider who actually knows your name.

That has not changed. Every patient is seen by an experienced provider. Every protocol is adapted to the individual. And every decision is made right here in Santee — not by a regional manager in another state.
