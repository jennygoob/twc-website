Est. 2002

Serving Santee for Over Two Decades

THE WELLNESS CO. was founded right here on Mission Gorge Road by Dawn Philp, a board-certified Family Nurse Practitioner with a vision for something different: a clinic that treats the whole person, not just the symptom. What began as a single-provider practice has grown into East County San Diego's most comprehensive wellness destination.

More than twenty years later, that same provider-led philosophy defines everything we do. Every treatment plan is built by the people who administer it. Every protocol is adapted to the individual sitting across from us. This is not a franchise. It never has been.
