The system wasn't working. So she rebuilt it.

Dawn spent the early years of her career inside the conventional medical system. She followed the protocols. She prescribed what was recommended. And she watched her patients come back, again and again, with the same complaints — managed but never resolved.

Then it became personal. Her daughter Jennifer dealt with persistent health issues from a young age — gut imbalances, histamine reactions, weight struggles, even shingles at 20. Not life-threatening, but never explained. Never resolved. And consistently dismissed as "normal."

Why could one person handle stress, food, or environmental triggers with no issue — while another couldn't? What was missing at the foundation?

That question changed everything. Dawn stepped outside the system and rebuilt her entire clinical approach from the ground up. Root cause, not symptom management. Biomarker data, not guesswork. Optimal ranges, not "normal" ranges that represent population-wide averages of people who are already sick.

She studied functional medicine, integrative protocols, peptide therapy, and bioidentical hormone optimization — not because they were trendy, but because they answered the questions conventional medicine wouldn't even ask.

Jennifer grew up inside that process. She watched every assumption get challenged, every protocol get questioned. And over time, she didn't just get better — she understood why. Together, they built THE WELLNESS CO. — and then CLARITY, the diagnostic program that now anchors every patient's care.

Today, Dawn personally reviews every CLARITY lab panel. She connects patterns across 10 body systems that most providers would never think to cross-reference. And she designs the individualized protocols that her patients — from 25-year-olds to 70-year-olds — build their health around.
